## 🧟 What is *Tokyo Ghoul*?

**Tokyo Ghoul** is a dark fantasy anime and manga series created by **Sui Ishida**. It follows a college student named *Ken Kaneki* who becomes a half-ghoul after a chance encounter with a mysterious girl.

---

### 🔥 Main Themes

- Identity & duality
- Survival in a cruel society
- What it means to be human vs. monster
